# Paperclip icon v1

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Source artwork for the Android and iOS launcher icons introduced in v1.9.0. Platform sizes are generated by `tool/generate_app_icons.py`.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single square mobile app icon design for a calm minimalist personal bookkeeping app, SoAccount (素帐). Primary request: a white background with a document held by a paperclip; refine this simple idea into a polished, distinctive, very legible small-size icon. Create ONE finished icon, not a presentation board or multiple variants. Pure solid white edge-to-edge square background. Center one upright, softly rounded rectangular sheet of paper, with a small neatly folded upper-right corner. The sheet is a very light warm gray so it subtly separates from the white background, with a confident dark charcoal outline of consistent medium-heavy weight. An oversized but elegant dark muted forest-green paperclip visibly clips over the upper-left edge of the sheet: recognizable continuous rounded bent-wire paperclip silhouette, clear overlap and correct occlusion, with enough open interior space to remain readable at 32px. The document and clip form one compact balanced silhouette. On the lower half of the paper, just three short rounded horizontal strokes of different lengths suggest orderly ledger entries, with ample blank space; these are abstract marks, no actual writing or numbers. Flat vector-like geometric illustration, crisp clean contours, restrained and friendly, quiet visual character. Avoid hairline detail. The whole mark occupies roughly 65–70% of the square with generous even white margins and survives circular or rounded-square app icon masks. Nearly front-on, no dramatic perspective. A barely perceptible soft shadow under the page is acceptable, but keep the icon almost flat. No external rounded-square tile outline, no device mockup, no lettering, no currency symbol, no watermark, no decorative objects, no glossy gradients, no photorealism, no grid of options. Render high-resolution square artwork.
